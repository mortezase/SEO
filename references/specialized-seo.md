# Specialized SEO

## Local SEO

Keep business name, address, phone, hours, categories, services, and service areas consistent with reality. Build useful location pages with unique local value; do not generate doorway pages with swapped city names. Review profile completeness, review acquisition and response practices, local citations, map landing pages, LocalBusiness markup, and conversion tracking.

## International SEO

Define market and language ownership before implementation. Use stable localized URLs, self-referencing canonicals, reciprocal valid `hreflang`, and an `x-default` where appropriate. Avoid automatic redirects that prevent users or crawlers from reaching alternatives. Translate the experience, not only keywords, including currency, units, legal content, support, and search intent.

## Ecommerce SEO

Clarify indexation rules for categories, filters, sorting, search results, variants, discontinued products, and pagination. Preserve useful category copy without obstructing shopping. Keep product titles, descriptions, images, price, availability, shipping, returns, reviews, and Product/Offer markup accurate.

For unavailable products, choose among continued availability, alternatives, category redirection, or removal based on permanence, demand, backlinks, and user value. Do not redirect every retired product to the homepage.

## Programmatic SEO

Require a defensible dataset, genuine per-page utility, quality thresholds, indexation controls, update ownership, and consolidation rules. Avoid mass-producing near-duplicate pages without unique value.
