# Authority and links

## Internal links

Use crawlable descriptive links to connect related tasks, establish hierarchy, and help important pages receive appropriate prominence. Diagnose orphan pages, excessive depth, broken links, irrelevant sitewide links, ambiguous anchors, and link concentration caused by templates.

## Backlink analysis

Use sourced link data with a collection date. Separate raw links, referring domains, followed links, topical relevance, placement, estimated authority, and actual referral or ranking impact. Compare competitors only when the same provider, market, and time window are used.

## Acquisition

Favor link earning through useful assets, research, tools, partnerships, expert contributions, community participation, and legitimate digital PR. Outreach should be relevant, personalized, transparent, and compliant with platform and legal requirements.

Do not recommend paid-link schemes, private networks, automated spam, deceptive guest posting, hacked links, fake reviews, or manipulative anchor campaigns. Treat disavowal as an exceptional, evidence-heavy action rather than routine cleanup.
