# Quality gates

Before completing substantial SEO work, check the following.

## Evidence

- Findings identify the source, date, scope, and representative examples.
- Unknowns, estimates, and inaccessible systems are explicit.
- No traffic, ranking, volume, backlink, competitor, or AI-visibility data was invented.

## Technical correctness

- Redirects, status codes, canonicals, robots directives, sitemaps, internal links, rendered content, and structured data agree.
- Recommendations account for the actual framework and deployment model.
- Accessibility, privacy, security, analytics, and conversion behavior are not casually broken.

## Search quality

- Content serves a real user task and adds defensible value.
- Metadata and markup describe visible content accurately.
- Local, link-building, programmatic, and AI-search tactics avoid spam and deception.

## Actionability

- Each important issue has impact, priority, affected scope, owner or dependency, proposed remedy, and validation method.
- High-priority work is separated from optional polish.
- The final report states what was verified after changes and what remains untested.
