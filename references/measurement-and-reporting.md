# Measurement and reporting

## Measurement design

Tie SEO metrics to business outcomes. Define conversions, leading indicators, segments, attribution limitations, data owners, and reporting cadence before judging success.

Useful dimensions include query, landing page, page type, market, device, search appearance, brand/non-brand, new/returning, and conversion type. Compare like periods and account for seasonality, migrations, releases, campaigns, tracking changes, and search-platform updates.

## Diagnostics

When performance changes, separate demand, visibility, click-through rate, indexation, ranking, page mix, conversion, and measurement effects. Build a dated timeline and test competing explanations against segmented data.

## Prioritization

Score opportunities using expected impact, confidence, effort, dependency, risk, and time to evidence. Blockers and scalable template fixes normally outrank isolated cosmetic changes. Forecasts must show assumptions and ranges rather than false precision.

## Reporting

An executive summary should state what changed, why it likely changed, business impact, actions, owners, and next measurement date. Append methodology and evidence so specialists can reproduce the analysis.

Do not present correlation as causation or third-party estimates as first-party results.
