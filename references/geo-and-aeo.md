# GEO and AEO

Optimize for accurate extraction, attribution, and recommendation without weakening conventional search quality.

## Answerability

Make important questions easy to answer with concise statements followed by supporting detail, evidence, limitations, dates, and clear entity names. Use useful headings, tables, definitions, comparisons, and steps when they fit the task.

## Entity and trust signals

Keep organization, product, people, authorship, contact, policies, claims, and external profiles consistent. Provide primary evidence, original research, expert review, and transparent update dates where relevant.

## Crawler and agent readiness

Inspect access for major search and AI crawlers according to the owner's policy. Ensure meaningful content is available in rendered HTML, URLs are stable, citations resolve, and structured data matches the page.

Treat `llms.txt` as an experimental discovery aid, not a ranking requirement or replacement for sitemaps, robots rules, navigation, or accessible content. Do not claim guaranteed inclusion in AI answers.

## Measurement

Define a repeatable prompt set by audience and journey stage. Record engine, model or surface, date, location where relevant, answer, cited sources, brand presence, accuracy, sentiment, and competitor presence. Because outputs vary, use repeated observations and trends rather than one-off screenshots.
